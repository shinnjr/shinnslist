**What counts as an application-ready package?**  
A source-checked opportunity, documented eligibility, tailored answers assembled for the real application, supporting-item checklist, and explicit blockers for any fact only your organization can provide.

**Why a $99 deposit?**  
You review real work first. The deposit reserves founding delivery capacity and applies to the $499 annual total.

**What if the preview finds no credible match?**  
You do not pay. We would rather show no fit than waste your reputation on a weak application.

## Positive-reply handling

### “Yes, send it”

Great. I will use only public organizational facts for the first pass and mark every unknown rather than guessing. I will send three source-backed matches and one application preview; you can correct the profile before anything is used. No card is required.

### “What is the catch?”

There is none in the preview. We need real buyer evidence before treating the product or price as validated, so the founding group gets a prospect-specific preview first. If it saves real work, the paid offer is $499 for the year; if it does not, the feedback is still useful.

### “Do you have a grant writer?”

Shinnslist is an application-preparation workflow, not a claim that an AI replaces professional judgment. It preserves official sources and approved facts, assembles tailored application answers, blocks unknowns, and keeps consequential approvals with your organization. If an opportunity needs specialized legal, accounting, or program expertise, the package flags that rather than improvising.

### “Can you work on contingency?”

No. The offer is a flat preparation fee and is never a percentage of an award.

## Fulfillment checklist

1. Confirm official organization identity and public facts.
2. Build the minimum truth profile and label inferred versus verified facts.
3. Find and re-verify at least three opportunities from official sources.
4. Run hard eligibility and fee checks before drafting.
5. Pick the best near-term opportunity and capture every real application question.
6. Draft only from supported facts; block unknowns.
7. Produce a concise effort checklist and deadline/obligation summary.
8. QA source links, claims, dates, numbers, and approval boundary.
9. Deliver the preview; log viewed/replied/deposit state.
10. After payment and required facts, deliver the first application-ready package within the stated service window.
