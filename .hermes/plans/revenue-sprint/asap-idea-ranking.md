## Why the winner clears the bar

- Live brand/product: `https://shinnslist.com/` returned HTTP 200 with the grant-autopilot offer.
- Buyer pool: IRS-derived analysis found 1,959 Denver-metro charities in the target band, including 1,049 Denver-city addresses.
- Demand mechanism: qualitative user evidence says completed applications are more valuable than another database; the human approval boundary directly addresses the spam/reputation objection.
- Price logic: official competitors observed on 2026-08-11 sell grant research/drafting at roughly $32–$349/month; the market ledger recommends testing a bounded $499 annual offer.
- Honest gap: no representative buyer has paid or deposited. Therefore the first work is a paid evidence test, not more software.

## Selected evidence test

1. Show a prospect-specific free preview: three official-source matches plus one application preview with unknown facts blocked.
2. Ask for a $99 deposit that applies to a $499 annual plan; collect the remaining $400 after review of the first application-ready package.
3. Pass within seven days on one $499 sale or two $99 deposits.
4. Stop or repackage after 50 qualified delivered outreaches and eight completed previews produce zero deposits and fewer than five substantive positive replies.
