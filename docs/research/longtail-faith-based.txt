NOTES FOR SHINNSLIST USE
- Most faith-based emergency assistance is DELIVERED LOCALLY (diocese/presbytery/congregation/
  federation/relief center), so the URL above is the national body + locator, and the true
  grant lives at the local level. Route users: national body → local agency locator → apply.
- Strongest "long tail / didn't know I qualified" picks:
    • ICNA Relief + Islamic Relief Zakat (open to ALL faiths, very active, under-advertised)
    • Hebrew Free Loan Societies ($0-interest loans — families locked out of banks)
    • JFS/Jewish Family Services + local federations (emergency aid regardless of religion)
    • SVdP home-visit model + Catholic Charities find-help locator (Catholic network)
    • Baptist (Send Relief + CBF + ABHMS) and Presbyterian (PDA + Compassion) networks
- Faith scholarships cluster on ANNUAL cycles (ISF, ISNA, GBHEM, Episcopal Foundation,
  Presbyterian Foundation); Shinnslist should surface "cycle open" flags and route to the
  specific application page each season.
