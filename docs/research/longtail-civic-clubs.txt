# ===================== ROTARY =====================
Rotary District Grants | The Rotary Foundation / Rotary International | https://www.rotary.org/get-involved/our-programs/grants | Rotary & Rotaract clubs/districts (apply via local district governor); funds small-scale, short-term community needs | Small-scale, typically ~$1,000-$25,000 (amount set per district) | Annual, district deadlines vary (spring/fall) | National umbrella (curl 200) | Apply through the LOCAL district; district committee decides. Not individual — club-run. Rotaract clubs (young adults 18-30) also eligible.

Rotary Global Grants | The Rotary Foundation / Rotary International | https://www.rotary.org/get-involved/our-programs/grants | Rotary clubs partnering with an international club; projects in Rotary's 7 areas of focus | US$30,000-$400,000 (Foundation co-funds ~50%) | Rolling (submitted year-round; needs training + district approval) | National umbrella (curl 200) | For larger international projects with measurable outcomes; clubs must complete Global Grant training. Local clubs can team up to access.

Rotary Disaster Response Grants | The Rotary Foundation | https://www.rotary.org/get-involved/our-programs/grants | Rotary clubs/districts in natural-disaster-affected areas | Up to ~US$25,000 | Rolling on activation after a disaster | National umbrella (curl 200) | Relief + recovery; apply via district. Part of Rotary's grant portfolio.

Rotary Peace Fellowships | The Rotary Foundation | https://www.rotary.org/en/our-programs/peace-fellowships | Individuals w/ bachelor's + 3 yrs relevant work experience & leadership (grad students/professionals) | Full funding (tuition, room/board, travel) for master's or certificate program | Annual; apply ~May (2027-28 intake via local district by ~May 15) | National umbrella (curl 200) | Apply through a local Rotary district sponsor. 100+ fellows/yr worldwide.

# ===================== KIWANIS =====================
Kiwanis Children's Fund Grants | Kiwanis International / Kiwanis Children's Fund | https://www.kiwanis.org/childrens-fund/ | Kiwanis clubs, districts, Key Clubs & Circle K clubs running children-serving projects | Varies (grants to clubs/districts; plus Key Club/Circle K) | Annual / rolling | National umbrella (direct 403; VERIFIED via Wayback archive snapshot 2023) | Fund focuses on education/literacy, health/nutrition, youth leadership + maternal-neonatal tetanus/iodine. Apply via local Kiwanis club/district.

Local Kiwanis Club Scholarships | Individual Kiwanis clubs (nationwide) | https://www.kiwanis.org/kiwanis/childrens-fund/ (club locator: https://www.kiwanis.org/find-club) | Local high school & college students in the club's community | Varies (typically $500-$5,000) | Varies (usually spring, pre-graduation) | LOCAL (not centrally listed) | EVERY Kiwanis club runs local scholarships + supports Key Club (high school) & Circle K (college) students. Contact local club directly — this is the true long-tail moat.

Elks Grants & Appropriations (incl. emergency grants to lodges) | Elks National Foundation | https://www.elks.org/ENF/appropriations.cfm | Lodges & state associations for emergency & community needs | Varies | Rolling | National umbrella (curl 200) | Includes emergency grants to lodges after disasters/community crises.

# ===================== HOW LOCALS APPLY (the moat) =====================
# National umbrella sites define programs, but AWARD MONEY flows through LOCAL clubs/lodges/chapters.
# For each, the user should use the club's "Find a Club / Lodge / League" locator, then contact the
# local chapter secretary directly. Local club scholarship + community grant programs are almost never
# aggregated online — that is Shinnslist's differentiation.
# - Rotary: rotary.org/find-a-club
# - Lions: lionsclubs.org (Lion Locator)
# - Kiwanis: kiwanis.org/find-club
# - Elks: elks.org Lodge Locator
# - Optimist: optimist.org (find club near you)
# - Junior League: thejuniorleagueinternational.org (Find a League)
# - Altrusa: altrusa.org (find a club)
# - Zonta: zonta.org (club locator)
# - Pilot: pilotinternational.org (find a club)
# - Soroptimist: soroptimist.org (find a club)
