------------------------- FEDERAL / NATIONAL PROGRAMS -------------------------

1. Head Start (national) | U.S. Dept of Health & Human Services, Office of Head Start | https://www.acf.hhs.gov/ohs | Low-income families (income at/below federal poverty guidelines, or TANF/SSI recipient, or foster/homeless child); ages 0-5 | Free comprehensive early childhood education, health, nutrition, social services; no fee | ROLLING (local programs accept year-round; must apply at local grantee) | GOV | Apply via local Head Start center, not federally; find local center via childcare.gov. Family-services model — supports parents too.

2. Early Head Start | U.S. DHHS, Office of Head Start | https://www.acf.hhs.gov/ohs/early-head-start | Pregnant women & families with children under 3; low income per poverty guidelines | Free home-visiting and/or center-based care + family support | ROLLING (local grantees) | GOV | Pair with Head Start for age 0-5 continuum; many grantees have waitlists — apply early.

3. Child Care and Development Fund (CCDF) Subsidy | ACF Office of Child Care (federal) + State Lead Agencies | https://www.acf.hhs.gov/occ | Working/attending-school low-income families; most states at/below 85% of State Median Income (varies) | Subsidized child care (voucher paid to provider); family copay on sliding scale | ROLLING (state-specific; some states have waitlists) | GOV | CCDF is the umbrella — each state runs its own Child Care Assistance Program (see state entries below).

4. Child & Dependent Care Tax Credit | IRS | https://www.irs.gov/credits-deductions/individuals/child-and-dependent-care-credit | Families with earned income who paid for care of child <13 (or dependent/spouse incapable of care) so they can work | Up to $3,000 credit for 1 dependent / $6,000 for 2+ (covers up to 35% of qualifying expenses) | ANNUAL (claimed on tax return; deadline = federal filing deadline) | GOV | Refundable portion expanded; keep care receipts. Separate from Dependent Care FSA (can't double-dip).

5. Head Start / Early Head Start Center Locator & resources | U.S. DHHS / ECLKC | https://eclkc.ohs.acf.hhs.gov/ | Families of children 0-5 | Free Head Start/EHS program search | ROLLING | GOV | Use to find local Head Start grantee to enroll; also hosts training.

6. Benefits.gov — Child Care benefit finder | U.S. Government (SAVE) | https://www.benefits.gov/benefit/599 | Varies by benefit screened | N/A — screening tool | ROLLING | GOV | Screens families for all federal child care benefits incl. CCDF, Head Start, state subsidies.

7. SBA loans for child care providers (start-up/expansion) | U.S. Small Business Administration (7(a)) | https://www.sba.gov/funding-programs/loans | New/expanding child care businesses (centers, family child care homes, providers); for-profits | 7(a) loans up to $5M (guaranteed portion); also SBA child care-specific loan guarantee program | ROLLING (via SBA lenders) | GOV | PROVIDER START-UP GRANT/FINANCING category; SBA has special 7(a) delegation for child care. Not a grant — low-interest guaranteed loan.

------------------------- STATE CHILDCARE SUBSIDY / SCHOLARSHIP PROGRAMS ------

8. Waitlist demand exceeds funding.

9. Texas Child Care & Early Learning Program | Texas Workforce Commission | https://www.twc.texas.gov/programs/child-care | Low-income working families, students, and those receiving/providing child care; income ≤ ~85% SMI | Subsidized child care (voucher to provider); copay on sliding scale | ROLLING (apply through local workforce board) | GOV | Named state CCDF program; contact local Texas Workforce Solutions office.

10. Illinois Child Care Assistance Program (CCAP) | Illinois Dept of Human Services | https://www.dhs.state.il.us/page.aspx?item=32976 | Working low-income families; income up to 225% FPL | Subsidized child care; family copay based on income | ROLLING | GOV | Includes a "grant-funded" pathway; application via local Child Care Resource & Referral.

11. Pennsylvania Child Care Works | PA Dept of Human Services (OCDEL) | https://www.dhs.pa.gov/Services/Children/Pages/Child-Care-Works.aspx | Low-income working families; income up to 200% FPL | Subsidized child care (voucher); copay on sliding scale | ROLLING | GOV | PA CCIS network helps navigate; also Provider Pre-Licensing & Startup support available.

12. First 5 California (family-support + subsidized child care) | First 5 California (state tobacco-tax funded) | https://www.first5california.com/ | California families with children 0-5 | Funded child care/early learning, home visiting, parent resources via county First 5 commissions | ROLLING | NONPROFIT/GOV | Local county First 5 commissions award child care & family-support funds; amounts vary by county.

------------------------- PROVIDER START-UP / SUPPLY GRANTS (context) ---------
NOTE: For families looking to START a child care business (common Shinnslist angle), the main access points are the SBA 7(a) child care loan guarantee (above) plus state "child care supply/business" grant programs administered through the same state lead agencies (MD, TX, IL, PA listed above) — many states run recurring provider startup/stabilization grant rounds. Check state CCR&R (Child Care Aware) for current provider-grant application windows.
